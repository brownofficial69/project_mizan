# Clip permission request: template and process

General information, not legal advice. For anything commercial, check gov.uk / the UK Intellectual Property Office or ask a solicitor.

## Process (for the owner and the bot)

1. The bot may **draft** a request. Only the owner sends it, from the owner's own email or DM. The bot never sends messages on its own.
2. Be specific about what you will use and where. Vague permission is hard to rely on later.
3. Save the creator's written reply (email, or a screenshot of the DM with the date) in `docs/clipping/evidence/` and give it a file name that matches its row in `permissions_register.csv`.
4. Add or update the row in the register **before** the first clip is made.
5. Stay inside the scope they gave. If you want to go beyond it (more clips, another platform, monetisation), ask again.
6. If they say stop, or ask for a takedown, remove the clips promptly and record it in the register.
7. Re-check any permission with an expiry date. Treat "unknown" as "no".

## Email / DM template

Subject: Request to share short clips of your content (with credit)

> As-salamu alaykum / Hello [Name],  *(optional greeting; keep whichever suits you)*
>
> I run [DEEN DROP / your channel name], where I share short clips to [one-line purpose, e.g. introduce people to thoughtful talks]. I would like your permission to use short clips from your content.
>
> **What I would use:** [e.g. clips of up to 60 seconds from the video at LINK, or "clips from your public talks"]
> **Where I would post them:** [e.g. Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts]
> **How I would credit you:** [e.g. your name and a link to the full video in the caption and on-screen]
> **Monetised?** [Yes / No. If yes, say how, e.g. platform ad revenue, sponsorships, affiliate links]
> **Edits:** [e.g. trimming and captions only; no changes to what is said, and I would not use clips out of context]
>
> If you are happy with this, please reply to confirm in writing, and tell me anything you would like me to avoid. If you would rather I did not, no problem at all, and I will not use your content. You can ask me to take any clip down at any time and I will do so promptly.
>
> Jazakallahu khayran for your time.
>
> [Your name]
> [Channel / brand]
> [Contact email or handle]

## What a clear permission looks like

- Names who is giving it (the creator or their business) and confirms they own or control the rights.
- Says which content, which platforms, and whether monetisation is allowed.
- States any conditions (credit wording, no edits to meaning, expiry date).
- Is in writing and dated.

If the creator uses a manager, label or network, ask them to confirm the right person to approve it.

## Do not

- Treat a reply like "sure" with no detail as covering new uses later.
- Assume permission because a clip is public, popular or credited.
- Use footage from creators who have not replied or who said no.
